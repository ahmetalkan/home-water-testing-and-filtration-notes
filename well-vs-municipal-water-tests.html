Municipal vs Private Well Water Testing Checklist

Testing priorities look very different depending on whether water arrives from a municipal treatment plant or a backyard wellhead.

Water supplyBaseline data sourceCommon homeowner follow-up tests
Public municipal waterAnnual EPA-mandated Consumer Confidence Report (CCR)First-draw lead/copper test if older service lines or solder exist; hardness and chlorine/chloramine check
Private domestic wellHomeowner-ordered state-certified laboratory panelAnnual total coliform bacteria, nitrates, total dissolved solids, and pH; periodic iron, manganese, arsenic, or radon checks per local geology

EPA's private well program advises retesting a well after nearby flooding, septic issues, changes in taste or clarity, or mechanical pump repairs. Explore treatment differences in municipal vs well water treatment on Water Treatment Pro.
